Grand Treasure Hunt / 秘宝大作战

A clear F2P plan · World Tree Energy / 世界树能量 · reward choices
Reworked · EN + 中文Your T3 Arcanist

The simple rule: save your World Tree Energy until the Level 5 rewards are worth buying. Your energy carries over to the next event, but the reward ladder resets to Level 1 every week.

Recommended F2P default: save for one event period, then use roughly 4,440 energy to unlock Level 5 and claim two Level 5 rewards. Do this only when at least two of the available prizes are worth taking. Otherwise, keep saving.

1 · How the event works / 活动规则

  • The event runs in seven-day periods and opens from server day 8.
  • World Tree Energy / 世界树能量 carries over. You do not lose saved energy when the timer ends.
  • The reward ladder resets. Every new period starts at Level 1, so you must unlock the ladder again.
  • To move up, buy one reward at each of Levels 1, 2, 3 and 4. You do not need to buy every item shown at those levels.

Do not mix up these numbers: 2,040 only unlocks Level 5; it does not buy a Level 5 prize. One Level 5 prize costs 3,240 in total. The often-quoted 9,720 is a full-shop clear estimate (buying every item across all five levels), not the cost of reaching Level 5 or claiming its prizes.

3 · How much free energy do you get? / 每周免费能量

The English and Chinese guides estimate about 2,250 energy per week: around 2,240 from event tasks plus 10 from the free shop pack. Check your event task list and free pack, because the exact amount can change with the event version.

  • One week gives enough to unlock Levels 1–4 (2,040), but not enough to buy a Level 5 prize.
  • One Level 5 prize needs 3,240 total.
  • Two Level 5 prizes need 4,440 total, which is just under two weeks of free income (about 4,500).

7 · Chinese guide shorthand / 中文攻略里的数字

  • 0 = this event period, buy nothing and save the energy.
  • 4 = buy one item at each of Levels 1–4 only. Cost: 2,040. Level 5 is unlocked next time only after paying its ladder costs again.
  • 5 = buy one item at each of Levels 1–5. Cost: 3,240 total.
  • 2×5 = buy one item at each of Levels 1–4, then buy two Level 5 prizes. Cost: 4,440 total.
  • 3×5 = buy one item at each of Levels 1–4, then buy all three Level 5 prizes. Cost: 5,640 total.

So a cycle written 0 → 2×5 means: skip one period, then use the energy banked across both periods to take two Level 5 prizes in the next one. The Chinese guide also describes 0 → 5 → 5: skip one period, then take one Level 5 prize in each of the next two periods. Use that when you want to follow the Level 5 rotation across two different weeks rather than take two prizes from one week's menu.
