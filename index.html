Current priority: relics over skill unlocks

Your T3 Arcanist · current recommendation
Account-specificCurrent focus

All skills are unlocked, so shift the main progression focus toward relic collection and permanent collection bonuses. Skill stars still matter; don't abandon them entirely.

  1. Collect missing relics and complete useful sets.
  2. Invest in duplicates / stars where they improve useful bonuses.
  3. Level the five relevant Dark-element relic slots evenly toward the permanent seasonal threshold.
  4. Keep a reserve for meaningful star upgrades to your main DPS Techniques and Charms.
  5. Maintain enough seasonal gear power to clear content; avoid over-investing in levels that reset unless they unlock a reward or progression gate.
Season reset caution: relic collection and duplicates were reported as permanent, but relic levels above the season's permanent threshold may be reduced. Confirm the current season threshold in-game before spending heavily.

Events & limited resources

Plan around rewards, not habit.

Wheel of Destiny

Ticket use and wheel selection
Reward pool rotates

Older guide compared four wheel types: Skill, Relic, Fantomon and Currency. A previous recommendation ranked Skill and Relic wheels ahead of Fantomon and Currency, but this should be reassessed against your current account and exact jackpot rewards.

Now that all skills are unlocked, a Relic wheel may be more attractive when it offers useful Goddess Cards / relic rewards. Do not spend tickets solely because they are available.

Before committing, check: jackpot contents, number of tickets needed, whether the reward is selectable or random, and whether the event is time-limited.

Grand Treasure Hunt / 秘宝大作战

Save energy for milestone rewards
Historical arithmetic · verify event rules

Previous notes described a tiered event using Auroradrasil Energy / 世界树能量. The stated tier costs were L1 240, L2 400, L3 600, L4 800, and L5 reward 1,200.

Based on those stated costs, unlocking one L5 reward costs 3,240 total; two cost 4,440; three cost 5,640. The old guide estimated around 2,250 free energy per event, but that income and event recurrence need checking on your server.

F2P strategy: compare the exact L5 reward rotation and save across events only if the currency carries over. Never assume event energy carries forward without checking the event notice.

Guild Coins: spend or save?

Guild level 9 reported
Your accountShop inventory must be checked

Guild level 9 does not necessarily mean Guild Shop building level 9. The shop building level controls stock and unlocks.

Do not hoard all Guild Coins for an unconfirmed event currency exchange. Spend on items that directly advance your current goals, but preserve a reserve if the shop has a limited weekly item you specifically need.

Older advice suggested Rare Goddess Cards and a weekly Lucky Ticket. Treat the earlier prices and stock counts as unverified until you inspect your current shop.

Before spending: compare Guild Shop items against your current relic needs, Fantomon plan and weekly limits. A Lucky Ticket is only worth buying when the Wheel of Destiny reward pool justifies it.
