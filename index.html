Grand Treasure Hunt / 秘宝大作战

Bilingual event guide · World Tree Energy, reward tiers, F2P cycles and relic priorities
Expanded guide · EN + 中文Personalized for your T3 Arcanist

Names: Grand Treasure Hunt (English/global name) = 秘宝大作战 (Chinese name). The event currency is World Tree Energy / 世界树能量; Prydwen's English guide calls it Auroradrasil Energy. Each period lasts seven days and the event first opens on server day 8.

The two rules that matter most: unused World Tree Energy carries into the next period, but the reward-tier progress resets and you start again at Level 1. Save the currency, not the tier unlocks. Do not spend just because the weekly event has refreshed.

1 · Energy and unlock costs / 能量与解锁规则

One reward purchase at each of Levels 1–4 unlocks the next level. You do not have to buy every reward at a level to progress.

TierCost for one rewardWhat it unlocksChinese label

Level 51,200 per prizeTop-tier reward; no further tier第5层 / 封印宝藏

Minimum route to one Level 5 prize: 240 + 400 + 600 + 800 + 1,200 = 3,240 energy. The first four purchases cost 2,040; the Level 5 prize itself costs another 1,200.

For comparison, two Level 5 prizes in one period cost 4,440 in total (2,040 + 2 × 1,200); three cost 5,640. The Chinese guide also reports 9,720 to clear every reward across all five tiers in its three-choice-per-tier layout: 3 × (240 + 400 + 600 + 800 + 1,200). These are different targets—do not confuse a full shop clear with buying one, two or three Level 5 prizes.

Free income: about 2,250 energy per week—2,240 from event tasks plus 10 from the free shop gift pack. That is not enough to reach a Level 5 prize from zero in one week, but two weeks provide about 4,500.

Notation from the Chinese guide: 0 = do not spend this period; 5 = take one reward at each tier, including one Level 5 prize; 2×5 = take two Level 5 prizes after unlocking the tier. The exact prize menu changes, so only use the burst plan when the event actually offers two top-tier choices you want.

  • Bank energy when the rotating Level 5 relic is low value for your account.
  • Spend when a limited relic or selection chest appears, or when the accumulated balance reaches the cost of a reward you have already decided to take.
  • Don't buy extra Level 1–4 rewards just to feel that the event currency was used. One purchase per tier is enough to reach Level 5.
  • Never assume a reward or energy balance resets just because the event timer ends. Currency carries over; the tier ladder starts over.

The active season's box description overrides this historical list.
Global schedule snapshot · 20 Nov 2026: community event trackers list the 13 Nov cycle as Primal Gem / Philosopher's Stone and the 20 Nov cycle as a Mythic Relic Selection Chest for Aethyris III. This is a cross-check for the English/global schedule, not proof that every regional server has the same event number or reset time. Your in-game reward panel is authoritative.

6 · Your T3 Arcanist: recommended decision order / 你的账号优先级

  1. Lucky Statue, if missing. A recurring chance to double dungeon appraisal rewards compounds over time and is a strong first event-exclusive relic target.
  2. Primal Gem, if its eligible gem sources are still part of your routine. It is a long-term bonus, but don't overrate it if your current progression no longer uses the affected gem sources.
  3. Time Sand / production relics that match your real bottleneck. Endless Hourglass may fit your relic progression; Golden Sacred Tree, Philosopher's Stone, Treasure Detector or newer seasonal relics are choices only when you actively benefit from their output.
  4. Mythic Relic Selection Chest. Hold it until it completes a region collection or selects a stat relic you actually need.
  5. 180 skill fragments. You already unlocked all skills, so don't take this for unlocking. Take it only if the tier matches one of your main Arcanist Techniques/Charms and the fragments make a real star/quality upgrade. Confirm exact compatibility in the skill screen.
Account-specific adjustment: you previously said Rolla is easy to obtain from Material Realm, so a Rolla-production relic such as Lucky Cat Statue is lower priority for you than a bonus to a resource that is genuinely limiting progression. Re-evaluate if your Cart economy changes.

7 · Quick checklist before claiming / 兑换前检查

  • How much World Tree Energy do I have after today's tasks and free shop pack?
  • Does this week's Level 5 panel contain a limited relic/selection chest I still need?
  • Have I counted the 2,040 energy required to unlock Level 5 before budgeting the 1,200 prize cost?
  • Will this resource/relic help my current account, or is it merely available?
  • Am I buying a duplicate? If yes, how many fragments does the current tooltip actually return?
  • Will unused energy carry to the next event? Historical and current guides say yes, but re-check the event notice after major updates.
