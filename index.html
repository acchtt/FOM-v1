Relic progression / 古遗物养成

Collection, resonance slots, star upgrades and seasonal planning · EN + 中文
New bilingual guide · v1.2Personalized for T3 Arcanist

The short plan: collect useful relics first, complete valuable sets, focus your rare Element tokens on Dark / 暗, then level the four Dark resonance slots evenly. Do not pour every duplicate into one relic before you have filled useful gaps in your collection.

Three separate systems: Collection / 图鉴收集 gives you the relic and collection bonuses; Stars / 升星 improve a relic and its set-star progress; Resonance / 共鸣 is the level track paid with Chrono Sand and Rolla. A relic can be collected, starred and placed in a resonance slot — those are not the same action.

2 · Collection and set bonuses / 图鉴与套装

  • Relics come from different Regions / 国度 and five Elements / 五种元素: Light / 光, Dark / 暗, Wind / 风, Water / 水 and Fire / 火. A collection gives passive benefits; you do not have to wear every relic to receive its collection effects.
  • A normal set is built from four relics in the same set group and Element. The set and its rarity/region are shown in the in-game Relic Codex / 古遗物图鉴. Mythic sets can be split across two regions, so the final pieces may not be in the same region pool.
  • Completing a set unlocks its base set bonus. The reference database also lists higher milestones when all four pieces reach 3 stars and when all four are Awakened. Check the milestone wording on the set itself before spending shards.
  • Equipping a relic in its matching Element resonance slot adds a further slot bonus; this is separate from the collection bonus. A full set does not need to be equipped together just to activate its collection bonus.
Collection-first rule / 优先收集: the Chinese beginner guide recommends spreading pulls across more missing relics instead of turning one relic into a high-star copy too early. As a default for an F2P/light spender, 4 new useful relics usually give more account breadth than concentrating the same progress into 1 relic. Break that rule only when a star-up completes a valuable set milestone or upgrades a key relic.

3 · Resonance slots: how to level them / 共鸣槽怎么强化

  • There are four resonance slots for each of the five Elements — 20 slots in total. Slot levels use Chrono Sand / 时之砂 and Rolla / 罗拉.
  • Relics of an Element share that Element's resonance level. The English guide notes that unequipped relics are adjusted to the level of the lowest slot for their Element. That is why pushing only one slot far ahead is inefficient.
  • Raise the four slots in your priority Element together: bring all four to the same level before taking one slot much higher. Then move to the next Element when the first group or your account's other needs justify it.
  • Put your strongest/highest-star relics or the relics with the most useful special effects into the matching Element slots. The matching-slot bonus is an extra benefit; it does not replace collection bonuses.

For your account: build the four Dark / 暗 slots first. The Chinese guides recommend Dark for Sage (贤者), while the current English T3 Arcanist guide says most of the class's T3 damage is Dark and Dark boosts strengthen the class's damage profile. After that, widen your other Elements instead of abandoning their collection bonuses.

4 · Star upgrades and duplicate shards / 重复遗物与升星

Duplicates provide the relic's own shards. Shards are used to combine/activate a relic and raise its stars. Same-quality universal relic shards can normally replace missing own shards one-for-one, but some event relics are restricted to their own shards. Awakening is the sixth star and has an additional universal-shard requirement.

F2P shard policy:

  • Do not automatically star every duplicate as soon as it appears. First ask whether the duplicate completes a set or reaches a useful star milestone.
  • Save universal shards for high-value relics whose own shards are slow or unavailable. Do not spend universal shards on a low-impact relic just to increase Power.
  • Prioritize a strong set's 3-star milestone before chasing Awakened status across multiple pieces. Awakening all four pieces is a long-term, expensive target — not an early F2P requirement.

5 · Where to get a missing relic / 去哪里获取缺失遗物

  1. Open Relic Codex / 古遗物图鉴, select the missing relic or set, then open its source information. Do this before spending a ticket or Destiny Fruit / 命运果实.
  2. Region / Kingdom pools (国度遗物池): use the pool shown for relics tied to that region. Compare the missing pieces in the pool rather than pulling blindly from the newest region.
  3. Element pools (元素遗物池): these are the targeted route for Element-specific relics and affinity. The Chinese guides recommend choosing the Element your class mainly deals damage with; for your T3 Dark Arcanist, that is Dark.
  4. Map exploration (地图探寻): some relics or shards can be found in a named small area using Destiny Fruit. The Codex's source display tells you which area to search. Some regional shard drops also depend on activating the local Goddess Statue / 女神像.
  5. Duplicates and universal shards: use these to advance a specific relic after you have identified a worthwhile target, not as a substitute for checking the source of a missing collection piece.
Do not rely on old gacha guarantees: Chinese community guides list ticket colours and historical pity counts, but these are version-sensitive. This guide intentionally does not give fixed pull-cost or pity numbers. Read the current pool's rules and counter in-game before spending.

7 · Season levels and reset / 赛季等级与重置

  • Relic resonance has a normal progression cap and a seasonal level above that cap. The cap can rise between seasons/promotions; old guides use examples such as S1 +10 and S2 +13, but these are not guaranteed to describe your current season.
  • Seasonal levels above the current base cap reset at season transition. The Chinese season guide explains that seasonal growth contributes to the Season Development score and is converted in part into Primostars / 原初之星 for permanent bonuses.
  • Do not interpret this as every relic collection or every star being wiped. Collection and relic ownership are separate from the seasonal resonance overflow.
  • The English Prydwen beginner guide still mentions a +10 reset point, while the Chinese season explanation describes changing seasonal caps. Use the current Season screen and the relic level-up preview in your own server — not a fixed old +10/+13/+16 table — before investing heavily beyond the base cap.
Spending rule near season end: do not push resonance levels above the displayed permanent cap just because you have spare Chrono Sand. First check the Season Development scoring and the Primostar conversion shown in-game. Seasonal investment may still be worthwhile for score, but it is not the same as permanent relic-slot growth.

8 · Your practical progression order / 你的账号执行顺序

  1. Open the Dark collection first. Check missing Dark relics by set and source. Use rare Element tickets on Dark unless a specific non-Dark relic is needed to finish a high-value set.
  2. Finish useful collection bonuses. Prefer new useful relics and complete sets over concentrating early duplicates into one random 5★ relic.
  3. Raise the four Dark resonance slots evenly. Use Chrono Sand when this is your real progression bottleneck. Rolla is a lower constraint for you because you previously reported that Material Realm supplies it readily.
  4. Target the right relic effects. Look for Dark affinity, Effect Hit Rate and Elemental Mastery, then Crit Rate/Crit DMG where the available relics offer them.
  5. Star up selectively. Use duplicate and universal shards when they complete a valuable set-star milestone or strengthen a key relic. Keep awakening as a later goal.
  6. Broaden to other Elements. Once your Dark collection and resonance are in a healthy position, return to missing sets and account-wide utility relics in the other Elements.
  7. Review at every season transition. Re-check the displayed resonance cap and Primostar rules before spending on seasonal overflow.

2 · What carries over at the end of S1? / S1结束后保留什么

  • The item stays: your S1 gear does not vanish from your bag.
  • The S1 seasonal multiplier stops applying: when the server moves to S2, S1 Season Level no longer matches the active season. Its combat value drops sharply compared with current S2 gear.
  • No S1 → S2 affix transfer: community guides and the transfer guide explicitly warn that S1 equipment cannot be inherited by S2 equipment. Do not pay for an S1 item with the expectation of preserving its lines.
  • S2 begins a new gear cycle: start replacing old pieces with S2 drops from the first S2 dungeons/regions. A lower-rarity current-season item can be worth comparing against an expired S1 piece because the S1 seasonal bonus has expired.
  • Other permanent account progress is not the same as seasonal equipment power. Do not assume a gear reset means your relic collection, learned skills or acquired Fantomons are erased.

Simple S1 end-of-season rule: use S1 gear to progress through S1; do not hoard it for S2 inheritance. Keep valuable resources, not obsolete S1 affix donors.

3 · Upgrade order in S1 / S1强化顺序

Equipment enhancement upgrades the slot, not the individual item. When you equip a new piece in that slot, it inherits the slot's enhancement level. Enhancement uses Raw Ore / 粗炼石 and Rolla / 罗拉, with Refined Ore / 精锻石 needed at every 5-level breakpoint (for example +4 → +5 and +9 → +10).

  • For S1, the Chinese F2P equipment advice suggests keeping the five slots reasonably balanced as you work toward the S1 level-100 range. However, the resonance bonus itself is small; the English beginner guide says not to equalize every slot just for resonance.
  • Use +5 / +10 milestones rather than spending randomly one level at a time. Keep enough progress across the slots to satisfy seasonal missions and unlock the first gem socket.
  • For the general Sage upgrade order, Prydwen lists Armor → Weapon → Off-hand → Helmet / Shoes. Treat it as a starting order; if a specific slot is blocking a stage or a key DPS stat, adjust.
  • Do not over-invest in a piece just because it has a high displayed Power score. Compare its fixed main stat, secondary stat, random additional stats and set bonus.

4 · Upgrade order in S2 / S2强化顺序

S2 changes the socket breakpoint. The first socket opens at +1 and the second at +130. The Chinese gem guide recommends pushing the most important slots toward 130 first; after all five slots have their second socket, redirect your mining toward the resource needed for further slot growth.

  1. Start with your most important combat slots rather than raising all five equally just for resonance.
  2. For a Sage/Arcanist, use Armor → Staff → Orb → Helmet / Boots as a general slot-upgrade order, but check actual stage progress and build role.
  3. Reach +130 on the five slots over time to unlock the second gem socket on each. Don't spend scarce premium currency chasing a high-tier gem before the socket exists.
  4. After the second sockets are open, choose between pushing key slots further and raising the remaining slots based on your seasonal missions, gear drops and actual bottleneck.
Do not confuse this with relic resonance. Gear resonance bonuses are small and gear-slot levels are separate from the five relic Elements. Equalizing gear levels is not automatically the best damage upgrade.

7 · Gems: one socket in S1, two in S2 / 宝石镶嵌

  • S1: one gem socket per piece is available. Prioritize filling the five first sockets with useful, affordable gems rather than chasing a perfect expensive combination.
  • S2: the second socket opens at +130 enhancement. One additional socket becomes available in each later season; the reference table lists the next breakpoints at +160 and +190, but those are not S2 unlocks.
  • There are eight gem types. Basic gems cover ATK, DEF, SPD and HP; composite gems add secondary benefits such as damage boost, damage resistance, Crit or Healing Boost. Socket availability differs by gear slot.
  • For a DPS Arcanist, the Chinese gem guide's DPS-oriented Sage/Sorcerer approach is a reasonable starting point: combine offensive ATK/Speed/Crit options with your actual stat needs. For healing, the gem mix changes toward HP and Healing Boost. Do not assume one fixed 5-gem recipe is best for every server's market or your current stat sheet.
  • Gem stats scale from base stats and are one part of total gear value. Avoid paying a large premium for a tiny gem-tier difference while your slot levels or equipment quality are still behind.

8 · Season 2 affix transfer and refinement / S2词条继承与洗练

These mechanics begin with S2. Eligible pieces are Mythic or higher (Chinese guides commonly call these Gold/Red). Always verify the rarity requirement in the in-game transfer screen.

  • Same-season transfer: transfer affixes between the same equipment type only (Staff → Staff, Orb → Orb, Boots → Boots). The source item on the left is destroyed. The target item keeps its own main and secondary stats; the affix lines are what transfer. Same-season transfer is free of Divinecraft Stones, but it still consumes the source gear.
  • S1 equipment cannot be used as an S2 donor. This is a hard season mismatch, not a fee problem.
  • Cross-season transfer from S2 onward: the Chinese guide lists Divinecraft Stone costs by retained affixes: 1 line = 300, 2 = 1,200, 3 = 3,200, 4 = 8,000. Unticked lines become random. Check the current transfer preview before confirming.
  • Refinement: available on Mythic or higher gear. It rerolls unlocked affix lines. Locking a line itself is free; the roll cost rises with the number locked: 0 locked = 100 Stones, 1 = 300, 2 = 1,200, 3 = 3,200.
  • Unique affixes: a Unique effect only activates once across your gear even if multiple pieces carry it. Do not spend stones trying to duplicate a Unique line. Gear effects can stack with Techniques/Charms, but the gear effect itself is not treated as a Technique for Technique-triggered effects.
F2P / light-spender rule: keep a good S2 Mythic+ donor only when its affixes are genuinely worth transferring. Do not burn a perfect piece just to move one mediocre line. Start refinement with zero lines locked (100 Stones), and lock a line only when the expected improvement justifies the sharply higher cost.

A clear F2P plan · World Tree Energy / 世界树能量 · reward choices
Reworked · EN + 中文Your T3 Arcanist

The simple rule: save your World Tree Energy until the Level 5 rewards are worth buying. Your energy carries over to the next event, but the reward ladder resets to Level 1 every week.

Recommended F2P default: save for one event period, then use roughly 4,440 energy to unlock Level 5 and claim two Level 5 rewards. Do this only when at least two of the available prizes are worth taking. Otherwise, keep saving.

1 · How the event works / 活动规则

  • The event runs in seven-day periods and opens from server day 8.
  • World Tree Energy / 世界树能量 carries over. You do not lose saved energy when the timer ends.
  • The reward ladder resets. Every new period starts at Level 1, so you must unlock the ladder again.
  • To move up, buy one reward at each of Levels 1, 2, 3 and 4. You do not need to buy every item shown at those levels.

Do not mix up these numbers: 2,040 only unlocks Level 5; it does not buy a Level 5 prize. One Level 5 prize costs 3,240 in total. The often-quoted 9,720 is a full-shop clear estimate (buying every item across all five levels), not the cost of reaching Level 5 or claiming its prizes.

3 · How much free energy do you get? / 每周免费能量

The English and Chinese guides estimate about 2,250 energy per week: around 2,240 from event tasks plus 10 from the free shop pack. Check your event task list and free pack, because the exact amount can change with the event version.

  • One week gives enough to unlock Levels 1–4 (2,040), but not enough to buy a Level 5 prize.
  • One Level 5 prize needs 3,240 total.
  • Two Level 5 prizes need 4,440 total, which is just under two weeks of free income (about 4,500).

7 · Chinese guide shorthand / 中文攻略里的数字

  • 0 = this event period, buy nothing and save the energy.
  • 4 = buy one item at each of Levels 1–4 only. Cost: 2,040. Level 5 is unlocked next time only after paying its ladder costs again.
  • 5 = buy one item at each of Levels 1–5. Cost: 3,240 total.
  • 2×5 = buy one item at each of Levels 1–4, then buy two Level 5 prizes. Cost: 4,440 total.
  • 3×5 = buy one item at each of Levels 1–4, then buy all three Level 5 prizes. Cost: 5,640 total.

So a cycle written 0 → 2×5 means: skip one period, then use the energy banked across both periods to take two Level 5 prizes in the next one. The Chinese guide also describes 0 → 5 → 5: skip one period, then take one Level 5 prize in each of the next two periods. Use that when you want to follow the Level 5 rotation across two different weeks rather than take two prizes from one week's menu.

Before you spend / 兑换前检查

  1. Have I checked this period's actual Level 5 prizes?
  2. How much energy do I have now, including the free pack?
  3. Have I budgeted 2,040 to reopen Levels 1–5 before adding the 1,200 price of each Level 5 prize?
  4. Are the two prizes worth 4,440, or should I take one for 3,240 / save for a better rotation?
  5. For a duplicate relic or selection box, have I checked the current tooltip and fragment conversion?
